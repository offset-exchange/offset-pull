OFFSET PULL — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-05 · Offset Exchange LLC · offsetexchangellc@gmail.com

The short version

Offset Pull runs on your own computer. The addresses you look up and the county documents you save stay on your computer. Offset Exchange does not receive them, sell them, or share them.

What Offset Exchange receives

  • Purchase details — your name, email and payment status, through Gumroad, which processes payments. We never see your full card number.
  • License checks — the app sends your license key or license ID and the app version to Gumroad and to our update service, to confirm your plan is active and to deliver county fixes.
  • Support reports — only if you choose to send one. A support report lists the steps the app took; it contains no passwords or license key.

Where your address goes when you search

To find the right county, the address you type is sent to the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoder, and when you press Pull, to OpenStreetMap's public geocoder (Nominatim). The address is then entered on that county's own official website, exactly as you would by hand. Those services have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

We do not run advertising trackers in the app, sell personal information, or build profiles of people or properties. We do not collect the documents you pull.

Keeping data

Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires for tax and accounting. License records are kept while your license is active and for a reasonable period after. Everything the app saves on your computer is under your control — delete it any time.

Your choices

Email offsetexchangellc@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it where the law allows. Cancel your plan from your Gumroad receipt at any time.

Children

Offset Pull is a business tool and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.
